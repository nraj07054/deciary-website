how it works

every big decision is a guess.

Deciary shows you how good your guesses really are, using nothing but your own words.

1

Write

What you chose, what you expect, why, and how sure you are. A few short questions.

2

Get challenged

Two quick questions written for your letter, by AI that runs on your phone.

3

Seal

Your prediction is locked. Nobody can change it, including you.

4

Open

On the day you picked, read what you really thought next to what actually happened.

After a few opened letters, your own history starts talking.

  • How sure you said you were, against how often you were right.
  • Where your gut is reliable: money, career, family and more.
